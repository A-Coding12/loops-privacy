LOOPS

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026 · Applies to: LOOPS for Android, version 1.0.0 (com.loopsgame.zen)

Summary. LOOPS has no account system and no servers of ours. The developer collects no personal data, runs no analytics, and adds no tracking of any kind. Everything the game stores stays on your own device. The one part of the app that reaches the network is the advertising SDK — Huawei Ads Kit, which serves an occasional interstitial and an optional rewarded hint. This policy explains exactly what that means.

1. Information we collect

We collect nothing. LOOPS does not ask you to register, does not request an email address or phone number, and does not create a profile. We collect no personal information, no usage records, no crash logs and no analytics, and we have no servers that could receive them.

The only component in the app that transmits anything at all is the advertising SDK described in sections 4 to 6. It is operated by Huawei, not by us, we have no access to what it handles, and none of it reaches us.

2. Data stored on your device

The game keeps your progress so that it is there when you reopen the app. This data is written to the app's own private storage on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

StoredPurpose
Game progress Current level, unlocked levels, and per-level star ratings
Best scores Your best result on each board
Sound settings Mute state, sound effects toggle, and music volume
Board seed A random number that makes your boards identical every time you play, so your progress stays consistent

None of this is linked to you, and none of it leaves your device. It contains no name, contact details, location, or identifiers.

3. Permissions

Version 1.0.0 requests the following Android permissions:

  • VIBRATE — used only for the optional haptic tap feedback. This is a normal permission: Android does not show a prompt for it, and it grants no access to any of your data.
  • INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE and BROADCAST_STICKY — used by the advertising SDK to request an ad and to learn whether one loaded. No game data travels on these connections.
  • QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES and REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES — declared by Huawei's advertising SDK as part of its standard integration. The game itself never reads your list of installed apps and never installs anything.
  • MANAGE_FOLD_SCREEN and MANAGE_FOLD_SCREEN_PRIVILEGED — declared by the advertising SDK for Honor foldable devices; they give the app nothing on other devices.
  • An app-scoped advertising component permission declared by the same SDK.

The app does not request access to your contacts, storage, photos, camera, microphone, location, or telephone information.

4. Network and data transmission

The game itself is offline by design: nothing you do in the game — your progress, your scores, your settings, your seed — is uploaded, backed up to us, or made available to anyone else. To move your progress to another device you would have to do so yourself; there is no built-in sync.

The app does include the INTERNET permission, and it serves one purpose: the advertising SDK in section 6 fetching an ad and reporting back that the ad was shown. No game data is attached to those requests, and none of your progress is ever transmitted.

5. Third parties

Version 1.0.0 contains exactly one third-party software development kit: Huawei Ads Kit, which provides the advertising in section 6. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporting, no attribution or marketing SDK, and no social login. We do not share data with third parties, because the game itself generates none to share.

6. Advertising

Version 1.0.0 contains advertising, served by Huawei Ads Kit. You may see a full-screen ad occasionally between boards, and you can choose to watch a rewarded ad in exchange for an extra hint. Both are optional — the interstitial can always be closed and the rewarded ad is never required, so nothing in the game is locked behind an ad.

The advertising SDK may request and use device and advertising identifiers in order to deliver and measure ads. That is handled by Huawei's ad platform under Huawei's own terms; the developer neither receives nor can access it, and we wrote no advertising or analytics code of our own — ad delivery is entirely the SDK's.

7. Children's privacy

LOOPS does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The developer collects no personal information from any user — of any age — and keeps none. Advertising is handled entirely by Huawei Ads Kit as described in section 6, and we have no access to anything it processes.

8. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete your progress: use the reset option inside the game, which removes the saved data immediately.
  • Delete everything: uninstalling the app removes all data it stored, including the save, because it is held only in the app's private storage.
  • Limit advertising: you can reset or opt out of personalised advertising using the Ads/Advertising ID controls in your Android settings. This changes which ads you see and has no effect on the game.
  • Decline nothing else: VIBRATE is the only permission Android lets you refuse in any meaningful way, and the game works with or without it. There are no cookies, no account to close, and no tracking of ours to switch off.

9. Security

Your progress is kept in the app's private storage, which Android isolates so that other applications cannot read it. The game never transmits it: none of your play travels over the network, so there is nothing of ours to secure, and we hold no copy of your data.

10. Changes to this policy

If the way LOOPS handles data ever changes, this page will be updated before or at the same time as the release that introduces the change, and the effective date above will be revised. The current version of the app is described accurately by this policy.
